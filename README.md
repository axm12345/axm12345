### 🔧 Cally: an AI agent that phones repair shops for you
**[Live demo →](https://cally-repair.vercel.app)** · [Code](https://github.com/axm12345/cally)

Say what's broken ("my iPhone screen is cracked"). Cally finds real repair shops near you, has an AI voice agent call them for a price and wait time, and shows you the quotes side by side. No more phoning five shops yourself.

`Next.js` `TypeScript` `Claude API` `ElevenLabs voice agent` `Google Places` `Vercel`

- Hold-to-talk voice input or typing, in English, Thai or Cantonese
- Pick a spot on the map and a 1–20 km radius; shops outside the radius are excluded
- Redesigned the first screen after user testing, because new users thought it was a chatbot

### 💬 LINE auto-reply bot for small businesses
[Code](https://github.com/axm12345/line-demo-cafe-bot)

Replies to customers on LINE automatically, in Thai or English. Common questions (hours, menu, delivery) get instant keyword answers. Anything else goes to Claude, which may only use the business's real info. If Claude isn't sure, the bot hands off to a human instead of making something up.

`Python` `Flask` `LINE Messaging API` `Claude API`
